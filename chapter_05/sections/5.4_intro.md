## The Unassociated Source Problem
\label{sec:5.4}

Having established the theoretical grounds for dark matter subhalo searches in the previous sections, we now turn to the observational reality. The Fermi-LAT catalogs contain a large population of gamma-ray sources with no identified counterpart at other wavelengths, and these unassociated sources have long been recognized as the natural hunting ground for DM subhalos. We begin by quantifying the unassociated population and the factors that govern association completeness, then survey the prior search efforts that have targeted this population.
