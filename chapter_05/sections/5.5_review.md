## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Missing quantitative specifics for prior shift magnitude
- **Location**: §5.5.2, ¶3 (prior shift paragraph)
- **Quote**: "The unassociated catalog therefore contains a larger relative fraction of Galactic sources---particularly pulsars---than the associated sample does"
- **Problem**: The chapter outline specifies concrete numbers: "Galactic sources (pulsars) constitute ~29% of unassociated vs. ~6% of associated at |b| > 10°". The draft uses only a qualitative statement. Quantitative anchoring strengthens the argument significantly and aligns with outline expectations.
- **Suggested fix**: Insert the approximate percentages.

### Issue 2: §5.5.2 covariate shift paragraph lacks citation for final claim
- **Location**: §5.5.2, ¶4 (covariate shift paragraph), last sentence
- **Quote**: "The spectral index and curvature distributions are also affected: the ratio of unassociated to associated source densities varies monotonically across portions of the (α, β) feature space, reflecting the flux-dependent completeness of the association procedure."
- **Problem**: This claim about monotonic variation is stated without citation. It originates from Paper 4 (specifically the KDE ratio plots in Figure 2 of Amerio et al. 2025) and should be cited.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `\cite{Amerio:2025fhz}` at end of sentence.
