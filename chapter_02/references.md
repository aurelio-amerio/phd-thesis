# Chapter 2: The Gamma-Ray Sky and Fermi-LAT — References & Sources

## 1. Reviews & Textbooks

### Fornasa & Sánchez-Conde (2015) — "The nature of the Diffuse Gamma-Ray Background"
- **Source**: arXiv:1502.02866 (Physics Reports)
- **Bib Key**: `DGRB-review`
- **Relevance**: The definitive review for the astrophysical gamma-ray sky. Systematically reviews every population contributing to the IGRB: blazars, SFGs, mAGNs, MSPs. Luminosity functions, spectral properties, and $dN/dS$ formalism. Primary reference for Sec. 2.2.

### Hooper (2024) — "Particle Cosmology and Astrophysics"
- **Source**: Textbook (002 review in NB)
- **Bib Key**: needs adding
- **Relevance**: Gamma-ray production mechanisms (Ch. 5): hadronic pion production, ICS (Thomson/Klein-Nishina), bremsstrahlung, synchrotron. Primary pedagogical reference for Sec. 2.1.

### Dermer & Menon (2009) — "High Energy Radiation from Black Holes"
- **Source**: Textbook (Princeton University Press), 002 review in NB
- **Bib Key**: needs adding
- **Relevance**: Isobaric and scaling models for $pp \to \pi^0$ production, $\gamma$-ray emissivity from $\pi^0$ decay (Ch. 8). Complements Hooper for Sec. 2.1.

### Cirelli, Strumia & Zupan (2024) — "Dark Matter"
- **Source**: arXiv:2406.01705
- **Bib Key**: `Cirelli:2024ssz`
- **Relevance**: Sec. 7 covers indirect detection with gamma rays; cross-references the MSP/GCE discussion.

### Pinetti (2022) — PhD Thesis
- **Source**: arXiv:2212.00125
- **Bib Key**: needs checking
- **Relevance**: PSF parametrization (Eq. 5.22–5.23), energy loss coefficient plots (Fig. 6.8). Used in Secs. 2.1.3 and 2.3.2.

## 2. Key Specific Papers

### Galactic Sources (Sec. 2.2.1)

- **Alpar et al. (1982)** — "A new class of radio pulsars"
  - **Source**: Nature 300, 728
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking (likely `1982Natur.300..728A`)
  - **Relevance**: Establishes the MSP recycling scenario. Foundational for MSP discussion.

- **Calore, Di Mauro & Donato (2014)** — "Diffuse gamma-ray emission from galactic pulsars"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1406.2706
  - **Bib Key**: `Calore:2014oga`
  - **Relevance**: MSP spectral properties and contribution to DGRB (< 1%). Key for MSP phenomenology.

- **Hooper et al. (2016)** — "The gamma-ray luminosity function of millisecond pulsars..."
  - **Source**: arXiv:1601.04325
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: MSP luminosity function constraints from globular clusters. Direct precursor to Paper 1.

- **Strong, Moskalenko & Ptuskin (2007)** — "Cosmic-ray propagation and interactions in the Galaxy"
  - **Source**: arXiv:astro-ph/0701517
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: GALPROP review. Standard framework for GDE modeling (Sec. 2.1.3 and 2.2.1).

### Extragalactic Sources (Sec. 2.2.2)

- **Ajello et al. (2015)** — "The Origin of the Extragalactic Gamma-Ray Background and Implications for Dark-Matter Annihilation"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1501.05301
  - **Bib Key**: `Ajello:2015mfa`
  - **Relevance**: Blazars = $50^{+12}_{-11}$% of total extragalactic emission. LDDE luminosity function.

- **Ajello et al. (2014)** — "The Cosmic Evolution of Fermi BL Lacertae Objects"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1310.0006
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: BL Lac luminosity function and evolution.

- **Di Mauro et al. (2014)** — "Diffuse γ-ray emission from misaligned active galactic nuclei"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1304.0908
  - **Bib Key**: needs adding
  - **Relevance**: mAGN contribution (~25%) to the DGRB.

- **Tamborra et al. (2014)** — "Star-forming galaxies as the origin of diffuse high-energy backgrounds"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1404.1189
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: SFGs contribute ~50% of DGRB at 0.3–30 GeV.

### IGRB, dN/dS, and Background Decomposition (Sec. 2.2.2)

- **Ackermann et al. (2015)** — "The spectrum of isotropic diffuse gamma-ray emission between 100 MeV and 820 GeV"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1410.3696
  - **Bib Key**: `Ackermann:2014usa`
  - **Relevance**: Definitive IGRB measurement. Baseline for all population models.

- **Abdo et al. (2010)** — "The Fermi-LAT high-latitude Survey: Source Count Distributions..."
  - **Source**: arXiv:1003.0895
  - **Bib Key**: `Fermi-LAT:2010tsy`
  - **Relevance**: First Fermi-LAT $dN/dS$ measurement.

- **Malyshev & Hogg (2011)** — "Statistics of gamma-ray point sources below the Fermi detection limit"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1104.0010
  - **Bib Key**: `malyshev2011statistics`
  - **Relevance**: Photon-count PDF method. Foundational for Papers 2 and 3.

### Gamma-Ray Production Mechanisms (Sec. 2.1)

- **Blumenthal & Gould (1970)** — "Bremsstrahlung, synchrotron radiation, and Compton scattering..."
  - **Source**: Rev. Mod. Phys. 42
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: Foundational leptonic cross-sections.

- **Kafexhiu et al. (2014)** — "Parametrization of the energy distribution of secondary particles..."
  - **Source**: arXiv:1406.6373
  - **Bib Key**: needs checking
  - **Relevance**: Modern $pp \to \pi^0 \to \gamma\gamma$ cross-section parametrization.

### Fermi-LAT Instrument & Data Products (Sec. 2.3)

- **Atwood et al. (2009)** — "The Large Area Telescope on the Fermi Gamma-ray Space Telescope Mission"
  - **Source**: arXiv:0902.1089
  - **Bib Key**: `Atwood:2009ez`
  - **Relevance**: Foundational LAT instrument reference.

- **Atwood et al. (2013)** — "Pass 8: Toward the Full Realization of the Fermi-LAT Scientific Potential"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1303.3514
  - **Bib Key**: `Fermi-LAT:2013jgq`
  - **Relevance**: Pass 8 reconstruction.

- **Bruel et al. (2018)** — "Fermi-LAT improved Pass 8 event selection"
  - **Source**: arXiv:1810.11394
  - **Bib Key**: `Bruel:2018lac`
  - **Relevance**: P8R3 event selection.

- **Acero et al. (2015)** — 3FGL Catalog
  - **Source**: arXiv:1501.02003
  - **Bib Key**: `Fermi-LAT:2015bhf`
  - **Relevance**: 3,033 sources.

- **Abdollahi et al. (2020)** — 4FGL Catalog
  - **Source**: arXiv:1902.10045
  - **Bib Key**: `Fermi-LAT:2019yla`
  - **Relevance**: ~5,000+ sources. Cited across all thesis papers.

## 3. References Breakdown by Section

### 2.1 Gamma-Ray Production Mechanisms
**Topics**: Hadronic (π0), Leptonic (ICS, Bremsstrahlung, Synchrotron), GDE model.

- **Hooper (2024)**: Ch. 5 — all production mechanisms (phenomenological)
- **Dermer & Menon (2009)**: Ch. 8 — $\pi^0$ decay emissivity
- **Blumenthal & Gould (1970)**: Foundational leptonic cross-sections
- **Kafexhiu et al. (2014)**: Modern pp cross-sections
- **Strong et al. (2007)**: GALPROP — CR propagation framework
- **Pinetti thesis**: Energy loss coefficients, bremsstrahlung/synchrotron/ICS
- **Fornasa & Sánchez-Conde (2015)**: Sec. 3 — GDE models applied to the ISM

### 2.2.1 Galactic Sources
**Topics**: GDE as foreground, Pulsars & MSPs, MSP luminosity function, GCE connection.

- **Fornasa & Sánchez-Conde (2015)**: Secs. 2.2.4 (MSPs), 3 (GDE)
- **Ackermann et al. (2015)**: Template decomposition for GDE separation
- **Alpar et al. (1982)**: MSP recycling scenario
- **Calore et al. (2014)**: MSP spectral properties, DGRB contribution
- **Hooper et al. (2016)**: MSP luminosity function in globular clusters
- **Cirelli et al. (2024)**: DM signal vs MSP spectral degeneracy

### 2.2.2 Extragalactic Sources
**Topics**: AGNs (blazars, mAGNs), SFGs, IGRB/EGB decomposition, dN/dS formalism.

- **Fornasa & Sánchez-Conde (2015)**: Secs. 2 (populations), 4 (UGRB statistics)
- **Ajello et al. (2015)**: Blazar contribution to EGB
- **Di Mauro et al. (2014)**: mAGN contribution
- **Tamborra et al. (2014)**: SFG contribution
- **Ackermann et al. (2015)**: IGRB spectrum measurement
- **Malyshev & Hogg (2011)**: Photon-count PDF for dN/dS
- **Abdo et al. (2010)**: First Fermi-LAT dN/dS

### 2.3 The Fermi-LAT
**Topics**: Instrument, PSF, IRFs, Pass 8, catalogs, TS definition.

- **Atwood et al. (2009)**: Instrument design
- **Atwood et al. (2013)**: Pass 8
- **Bruel et al. (2018)**: P8R3
- **Pinetti thesis**: PSF parametrization
- **Acero et al. (2015)**: 3FGL
- **Abdollahi et al. (2020)**: 4FGL
