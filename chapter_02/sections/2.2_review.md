Section 2.2 covers the necessary Galactic and Extragalactic source populations and correctly bridges to Chapters 4 and 5. The physics content is accurate and well-cited. However, the prose suffers from AI-pattern inflation (significance language, copula avoidance, rule-of-three structures), several coverage gaps relative to `chapter_outline.md`, and some citation inconsistencies against `references.md`.

Needs revision

## Critical Issues (🔴)

### Issue 1: Section 2.2.1 is drastically under-length
- **Location**: 2.2.1 as a whole
- **Problem**: `chapter_outline.md` allocates ~5 pages to 2.2.1, split across three sub-topics: Galactic Diffuse Foreground (~1.5 pp), Pulsars & MSPs (~3 pp), and Other Galactic Sources (~0.5 pp). The current draft is ~4 paragraphs (~1.5 pages), compressing GDE and MSP content together and missing the MSP luminosity function discussion entirely.
- **Missing content** per outline:
  - MSP luminosity function: mean $\langle L_\gamma \rangle \sim 10^{33}$–$10^{34}$ erg/s, the observable that determines whether MSPs can explain the GCE
  - Spatial distribution details: natal kicks, bulge vs. plane vs. globular cluster populations
  - GDE template decomposition: how Fermi-LAT separates it from point sources (recap of 2.1.3)
  - IEM model dependence and alternative templates
- **Suggested fix**: Extend 2.2.1 to ~5 paragraphs minimum. Add a dedicated paragraph on the MSP luminosity function and its role. Expand the GDE paragraph to discuss template alternatives and IEM dependence.

### Issue 2: Missing citation for Malyshev & Hogg bib key
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraphs 4–5
- **Quote**: `\cite{Malyshev:2011zi}`
- **Problem**: `references.md` lists the bib key as `malyshev2011statistics`, not `Malyshev:2011zi`. Using the wrong key will cause an undefined-reference error in LaTeX.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace `Malyshev:2011zi` → `malyshev2011statistics` everywhere.

## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 3: Missing reference — Hooper et al. (2016)
- **Location**: 2.2.1, paragraph 4
- **Problem**: The outline lists `Hooper et al. (2016)` as a key reference for the MSP luminosity function in globular clusters. The current draft mentions this approach but does not cite it.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `\cite{Hooper:2016rap}` (or whatever the correct key turns out to be) when mentioning MSP LF measurement in globular clusters.

### Issue 4: Missing references — Di Mauro et al. (2014) and Tamborra et al. (2014)
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraph 3
- **Problem**: The outline and `references.md` list Di Mauro et al. (2014) for the mAGN contribution and Tamborra et al. (2014) for SFGs. The draft cites only `DGRB-review` and `Ajello:2015mfa` throughout, missing these primary sources.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `\cite{DiMauro:2013xta}` after the mAGN 20–25% claim and `\cite{Tamborra:2014xia}` after the SFG 10–30% claim.

### Issue 5: AI-pattern — copula avoidance and significance inflation in 2.2_intro
- **Location**: 2.2_intro, line 3
- **Quote**: "Understanding the precise astrophysical origins, spatial morphologies, and energy spectra of these conventional emitters is paramount."
- **Problem**: "is paramount" is significance inflation (humanizer pattern #1). The sentence also uses a rule-of-three ("origins, spatial morphologies, and energy spectra").
- **Suggested fix**: "A detailed understanding of the astrophysical processes behind these conventional emitters is therefore necessary."

### Issue 6: AI-pattern — "formidable background" and "exhaustive and rigorous"
- **Location**: 2.2_intro, line 3
- **Quote**: "every ordinary astrophysical signal constitutes a formidable background. Consequently, isolating the subtle, elusive signature of dark matter annihilation fundamentally requires an exhaustive and rigorous characterization"
- **Problem**: "formidable", "subtle, elusive", "exhaustive and rigorous" are promotional/inflated adjectives (humanizer pattern #4). "Fundamentally requires" is filler.
- **Suggested fix**: "every ordinary astrophysical signal is a potential background. Isolating any dark matter contribution therefore requires a thorough characterization"

### Issue 7: AI-pattern — "heterogeneous collection" and "pervasive diffuse foreground"
- **Location**: 2.2.1, paragraph 1
- **Quote**: "a pervasive diffuse foreground and a heterogeneous collection of individual point sources"
- **Problem**: "Pervasive" and "heterogeneous collection" are inflated. A simpler construction is more natural.
- **Suggested fix**: "a diffuse foreground and a diverse population of individual point sources"

### Issue 8: AI-pattern — "fundamentally different physical mechanisms"
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraph 3
- **Quote**: "Star-forming galaxies produce gamma rays through fundamentally different physical mechanisms"
- **Problem**: "fundamentally different" is hedging inflation; the mechanisms were already explained in 2.1.
- **Suggested fix**: "Star-forming galaxies produce gamma rays through hadronic interactions, as described in Section 2.1.1"

### Issue 9: Transition missing between 2.2.1 and 2.2.2
- **Location**: End of 2.2.1 / start of 2.2.2
- **Problem**: The outline prescribes a Galactic → Extragalactic transition. 2.2.1 ends abruptly with "subdominant backgrounds" and 2.2.2 opens with "Beyond the Milky Way" which is adequate but could be stronger. The outline's transition sentence is: "the complexity of this sky — overlapping populations, diffuse foregrounds, unresolved sources — places stringent demands on the instrument" (at the end of 2.2 leading into 2.3). There should be a lighter transition between the two subsections.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a brief closing sentence to 2.2.1 that pivots outward: "Having surveyed the Galactic foreground and its point-source populations, we now turn to the extragalactic sky."

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 10: "abstract constraints" — wrong word
- **Location**: 2.2.1, paragraph 4
- **Quote**: "we abstract constraints that can be extrapolated"
- **Problem**: "Abstract" as a verb is unusual here and reads oddly. Likely meant "extract" or "derive".
- **Suggested fix**: "we derive constraints that can be extrapolated"

### Issue 11: "pronounced heavy tail" — redundant
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraph 5
- **Quote**: "a non-Poissonian distribution characterized by a pronounced heavy tail"
- **Problem**: "Pronounced" is unnecessary before "heavy tail" (redundant intensifier).
- **Suggested fix**: "a non-Poissonian distribution with a heavy tail"

### Issue 12: "exceptionally stringent constraints" — inflation
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraph 3
- **Quote**: "establishing exceptionally stringent constraints"
- **Problem**: "Exceptionally stringent" is significance inflation.
- **Suggested fix**: "establishing tight constraints"

### Issue 13: Em dash density
- **Location**: Multiple (2.2_intro, 2.2.1 para 1, 2.2.2 paras 1 and 3)
- **Problem**: Four em dashes across ~2 pages. Not egregious but borderline for the humanizer threshold. Consider converting one or two to parenthetical clauses or commas.

### Issue 14: "the accurate classification and modeling of blazars form a central pillar"
- **Location**: 2.2.2, paragraph 2
- **Problem**: "Central pillar" is significance inflation (humanizer pattern #1).
- **Suggested fix**: "the classification and modeling of blazars are central to the research presented in Papers 2, 3, and 4"
