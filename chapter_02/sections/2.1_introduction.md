## 2.1 Gamma-Ray Production Mechanisms

Astrophysical gamma rays from the Milky Way constitute the primary background in the search for dark matter annihilation signals. Before cataloging individual point sources or extracting the faint extragalactic glow, one must understand the physical processes that generate this intense foreground. 

This section reviews the standard gamma-ray production mechanisms. These processes fall into two broad categories: hadronic interactions involving cosmic-ray nucleons colliding with interstellar gas, and leptonic interactions involving energetic electrons and positrons scattering off radiation fields and gas. Operating in different energy regimes and tracing distinct target environments, they combine to produce the macroscopic Galactic Diffuse Emission that dominates the observed gamma-ray sky.
