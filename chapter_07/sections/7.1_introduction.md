\label{sec:threshold_limits}

Before describing how the source-count distribution can be leveraged to build a probabilistic catalog, it is useful to examine more closely the limitations of the standard approach. The fixed-threshold paradigm that underpins the \textit{Fermi}-LAT catalogs has been used effectively for over a decade, but it involves two distinct costs: a spatially non-uniform detection significance and the wholesale discarding of sub-threshold information. We address these in turn.
