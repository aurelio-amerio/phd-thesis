## Important Issues (🟡)
### Issue 1: Missing 1pPDF Context References
- **Location**: §7.2.1, expected per `references.md`
- **Problem**: The outline and `references.md` list `Malyshev:2011zi` (1pPDF) and `Zechlin:2015wdz` as "Photon count statistics context" for §7.2.1. These are not cited.
- **Assessment**: The draft handles this context through cross-references to Ch. 6, which is where 1pPDF methods are discussed in detail. This is a defensible editorial choice in a thesis where the reader proceeds sequentially. However, adding a parenthetical citation when mentioning "photon-count statistics" in the section intro would strengthen provenance without disrupting flow.
- **Suggested fix**: In `7.2_introduction.md`, consider: "...measurable via photon-count statistics \cite{Malyshev:2011zi, Zechlin:2015wdz}" — just the citation, no additional text.
