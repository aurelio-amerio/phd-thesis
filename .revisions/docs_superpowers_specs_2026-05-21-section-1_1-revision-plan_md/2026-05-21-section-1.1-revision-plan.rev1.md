### 3.2 Text edits — CMB paragraph rewrite (L100–111)

This is the biggest single edit. The current text jumps straight into acoustic peaks and Cℓ physics without grounding. Restructure as:

1. **Opening context** (new, ~3–4 sentences): The CMB is the thermal relic radiation from ~380,000 years after the Big Bang, when the universe cooled enough for protons and electrons to combine into neutral hydrogen — an epoch called *recombination*. Before this, baryonic matter was a hot plasma tightly coupled to photons. After recombination, photons streamed freely — this "last scattering surface" is what we observe as the CMB. Its temperature is remarkably uniform (~2.725 K) but carries tiny anisotropies at the level of ~10⁻⁵.

2. **Angular power spectrum intuition** (rewrite of L104–108, ~3–4 sentences): These anisotropies are decomposed into angular scales via the power spectrum Cℓ. Peaks in Cℓ correspond to sound waves in the pre-recombination plasma that were caught at maximum compression or rarefaction at the moment of decoupling. The heights and positions of these peaks encode the matter-energy content of the universe.

3. **Baryon vs. DM signatures** (revised from current L105–108, ~3–4 sentences): Keep the physical content about odd/even peaks and the third peak, but unpack more slowly. Baryons enhance compressions (odd peaks). DM sets the gravitational potential depth, modulating successive peak ratios. A universe without CDM would have a suppressed third peak — contradicted by Planck data.

4. **Planck results** (keep L108 mostly as-is): The quantitative Ω_c h² and Ω_b h² values.

5. **Remove CMB lensing paragraph** (L109–110): As per comment C3 — too technical, not essential for the DM abundance argument.
