## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Auto-correlation SNR threshold dependence
- **Location**: 8.3.3, paragraph 2
- **Quote**: "SNR ≃ 0.9 in auto-correlation"
- **Problem**: Paper 5 shows this SNR is strongly threshold-dependent (ranges from 0.9 at nominal 50h sensitivity to ~5.1 when degraded to 3h-equivalent detection). The sentence presents 0.9 as THE auto-correlation number without this caveat.
- **Fix applied**: Added parenthetical "(at the nominal 50-hour point-source detection threshold)" to clarify the conditionality.
