## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: DM flux multiplier $\Delta^2(z)$ introduced without definition
- **Location**: 8.2.1, paragraph 3
- **Quote**: "...is the flux multiplier that captures the enhancement from DM clustering and substructure"
- **Problem**: $\Delta^2(z)$ is used in the displayed equation but never defined or cross-referenced to where it is defined (Section 1.4 covers halo profiles and J-factors; an explicit cross-reference is missing). A reader seeing this equation for the first time may not know where to find its derivation.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `(see Section~\ref{sec:1.4})` after "DM clustering and substructure," or add "as defined in Section 1.4."
- **Status**: Applied ✅

### Issue 2: Cosmic shear paragraph (8.2.3) is slightly orphaned
- **Location**: 8.2.3, paragraph 5 (Cosmic shear)
- **Quote**: "Cosmic shear provides a complementary gravitational tracer."
- **Problem**: The paragraph on cosmic shear appears after the historical Fermi-LAT detection narrative and before the CTAO forward-look. Its connection to 2MASS/2MRS (the "golden channel" of the subsubsection title) is not made explicit. The reader may wonder why shear is mentioned in a section nominally about galaxy catalogs.
- **Suggested fix**: Add one sentence introducing it as an alternative to galaxy catalogs that shares some of the same advantages: "Beyond galaxy number counts, gravitational lensing maps offer an alternative tracer with independent systematics."
- **Status**: Applied ✅
