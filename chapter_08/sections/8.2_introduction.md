## 8.2 The Cross-Correlation Technique
\label{sec:8.2}

The angular power spectrum introduced in Section~\ref{sec:3.5} provides the mathematical language. What remains is to demonstrate why cross-correlating the gamma-ray sky with a galaxy catalog offers a practical handle that the auto-correlation and spectral analyses alone cannot match. The argument rests on three interlocking ideas: the redshift selectivity conferred by window function overlap (Section~\ref{sec:8.2.1}), the favorable noise properties that follow from combining two physically independent observables (Section~\ref{sec:8.2.2}), and the specific choice of low-redshift catalogs that maximizes both effects simultaneously (Section~\ref{sec:8.2.3}).
