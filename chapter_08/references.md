# Chapter 08: Cross-Correlations and Future Prospects (CTA) - References & Sources

## 1. Reviews & Textbooks
*General consensus, theoretical foundations, and state-of-the-art summaries.*

### Fornengo & Regis (2014) - "Particle dark matter searches in the anisotropic sky"
*   **Source:** **Priority: arXiv:1312.4835**
    *   **Relevance:** A comprehensive review of the Angular Power Spectrum (APS) formalism for Dark Matter searches. It details the "Halo Model" approach for calculating the 3D power spectrum and projecting it to the 2D sky, covering the full multi-wavelength spectrum from radio to gamma-rays.

### Fornasa & Sánchez-Conde (2015) - "The nature of the Diffuse Gamma-Ray Background"
*   **Source:** **Priority: arXiv:1502.02866**
    *   **Relevance:** (Same review as Ch. 6, but specifically **Sec. 5**). It discusses cross-correlations with independent probes (Galaxy catalogs, Lensing) as the primary method to filter out noise and separate Dark Matter signals from the Galactic foreground.

## 2. Key Specific Papers
*Primary sources for specific claims, historical limits, and experimental results.*

*   **Pinetti et al. (2025):** *Dark matter and galaxy cross-correlations with the Cherenkov Telescope Array Observatory*
    *   **Source:** **Priority: arXiv:2505.20383**
    *   **Relevance:** **(Paper 5)** The core paper for this chapter. It provides the sensitivity forecasts for the upcoming CTAO, demonstrating that cross-correlating TeV gamma-rays with low-redshift galaxy catalogs (2MASS, 2MRS) achieves sensitivities competitive with dwarf galaxy searches, specifically targeting the $z \lesssim 0.1$ window where DM peaks.

*   **Camera et al. (2013):** *Cross-correlation of Gamma-Ray Anisotropies and Cosmic Shear*
    *   **Source:** *arXiv:1212.5018*
    *   **Relevance:** The pioneering paper proposing the cross-correlation of the Extragalactic Gamma-ray Background (EGB) with Cosmic Shear. It establishes the key insight that shear (flux of mass) is a better tracer for DM than galaxies (flux of light), avoiding "bias" issues and providing a cleaner signal.

*   **Cuoco et al. (2015):** *Dark Matter Searches... via Cross-correlations With Galaxy Catalogues*
    *   **Source:** *arXiv:1506.01030*
    *   **Relevance:** A major experimental benchmark using Fermi-LAT data correlated with SDSS, 2MASS, and NVSS. It sets constraints on WIMP annihilation by fitting the observed cross-correlation signal with astrophysical models (blazars, star-forming galaxies), leaving little room for a DM component.

## 3. References Breakdown by Section
*Detailed mapping of which sections to read for each thesis part.*

### 8.1 & 8.2 Cross-Correlation Formalism
**Topics:** Angular Power Spectra ($C_\ell$), Halo Model, Window Functions, Cosmic Shear.

*   **Fornengo & Regis (2014):**
    *   **Chapter/Section:** Read **Sec 2**. The mathematical definition of $C_\ell^{\gamma g}$, including the Limber approximation and the role of Window Functions $W(\chi)$.
*   **Camera et al. (2013):**
    *   **Chapter/Section:** Read **Sec 3**. Why Cosmic Shear is the "golden channel" for DM cross-correlations.
*   **Ando et al. (2014)** *[arXiv:1312.4403]*:
    *   **Chapter/Section:** **Sec 2 & 4**. Discusses "Tomography"—using magnitude-limited (i.e., redshift-sliced) galaxy catalogs to map the DM distribution in 3D.

#### Additional Sources (Cross-Correlations)
*   **Xia et al. (2015)** *[arXiv:1503.05918]* - "Tomography of the Fermi-LAT signal" - detection of LSS signal > 3.5 sigma.
*   **Fornasa et al. (2016)** *[arXiv:1608.07289]* - Fermi-LAT Auto-correlation measurement (APS).
*   **Shirasaki et al. (2014)** *[arXiv:1404.5503]* - Early experimental attempt at Shear x Gamma correlation.

### 8.3 Looking Forward (CTA)
**Topics:** Cherenkov Telescope Array (CTAO), Next-gen sensitivity, Extragalactic Survey.

*   **Pinetti et al. (2025):**
    *   **Chapter/Section:** **(Paper 5)** Read **Sec 4 & 5**. Comparing the sensitivity of Cross-Correlation vs. Auto-Correlation for CTA, showing the factor of ~5 improvement due to background rejection.
