## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Overly heavy summary paragraph in §6.3.2
- **Location**: §6.3.2, ¶5 (lines 12)
- **Quote**: "The resulting framework addresses each of the four limitations..."
- **Problem**: This paragraph re-lists all four advantages in rapid succession, creating a checklist feel that partially duplicates §6.3.1. The "Most significantly" qualifier is a mild significance-inflation tell.
- **Suggested fix**: Compress the recap into one sentence and emphasise only the most distinctive aspect (amortisation). Move the note about "present analysis uses 1–10 GeV" earlier or remove duplication with §6.3.1's closing.

### Issue 2: Missing Compound Poisson Generator mention
- **Location**: §6.3.1
- **Problem**: The chapter outline's §6.2.2 mentions the CPG as an alternative methodology developed to "handle biases possibly present in the 1pPDF method" (Collin et al.). While the outline places it in §6.2, a passing acknowledgment in §6.3.1 would strengthen the limitations discussion by noting that the community has recognised these issues. Its omission could be read as presenting the CNN as the only alternative.
- **Suggested fix**: Add one sentence after the parametric-form paragraph noting the CPG as a prior attempt to address some 1pPDF biases.

### Issue 3: Likelihood described as "intractable" in §6.3.2
- **Location**: §6.3.2, ¶2
- **Quote**: "even though the corresponding likelihood is intractable"
- **Problem**: The 1pPDF likelihood is not strictly intractable — it is evaluated (with difficulty) by the analytical approach of §6.2. The word "intractable" overstates the case and contradicts §6.3.1, which describes the likelihood as "computationally demanding" rather than impossible. This mismatch could confuse the reader.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace "intractable" with "difficult to evaluate analytically under realistic conditions" or "computationally expensive to evaluate."

### Issue 4: Energy band mentions are scattered and partly redundant
- **Location**: §6.3.1 ¶6 (closing), §6.3.2 ¶5, §6.3.3 ¶3
- **Problem**: The note about using a single 1–10 GeV band and future multi-band extensions appears three times across the section. The first instance (§6.3.1 closing) is well-placed; the other two add little.
- **Suggested fix**: Keep the detailed statement in §6.3.1's closing paragraph. In §6.3.2 and §6.3.3, refer back briefly rather than restating.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 5: "the most precise measurements" — unsupported superlative
- **Location**: §6.3.1, ¶1
- **Quote**: "the most precise measurements of the sub-threshold dN/dS to date"
- **Suggested fix**: Soften to "the best available measurements" or simply "precise measurements."

### Issue 6: Sentence rhythm in §6.3.1 ¶2
- **Problem**: Three consecutive long sentences (30+ words each). The paragraph would benefit from one shorter sentence to vary the rhythm.
- **Suggested fix**: Split the sentence about inter-pixel correlations.

### Issue 7: "faithfully reproduces" in §6.3.2
- **Location**: §6.3.2, ¶2
- **Quote**: "faithfully reproduces the statistical properties"
- **Problem**: "Faithfully" is mildly promotional.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace with "reproduces" (the word is sufficient on its own).

### Issue 8: Bib key consistency
- **Problem**: Several bib keys remain unverified: `Gal:2017concrete`, `Cohen:2018spherical`, `Perraudin:2019deepsphere`, `Krachmalnicoff:2019nnhealpix`, `Tan:2021efficientnetv2`. These need checking against `bibliography.bib`.
- **Suggested fix**: User to verify during LaTeX compilation.

### Issue 9: §6.3.3 closing reference format
- **Quote**: "presented in the paper body (§\\ref{sec:6.7})"
- **Problem**: "paper body" is informal for a thesis. The text could use the chapter-internal language established elsewhere.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace with "presented in §\\ref{sec:6.7}" or "described in detail in the remainder of this chapter."
