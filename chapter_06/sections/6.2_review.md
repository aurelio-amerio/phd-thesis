## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Missing conceptual figure (§6.2.1)

- **Location**: §6.2.1
- **Outline spec**: "Consider including a schematic showing how dN/dS bridges the resolved catalog (bright end) and the unresolved background (faint end). → Conceptual diagram, not from a specific paper."
- **Problem**: No figure is present. The outline explicitly requests this.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a `\ref{fig:dnds_schematic}` placeholder or generate a conceptual figure. At minimum, add a sentence referencing the paper's Fig. 4 which shows the resolved dN/dS from 4FGL and the CNN reconstruction.

### Issue 2: Transition mismatch in §6.2.3 closing

- **Location**: §6.2.3, final paragraph
- **Quote**: "can we develop an inference method that reconstructs the dN/dS non-parametrically, efficiently, and with well-calibrated uncertainties — overcoming the limitations of the analytical 1pPDF approach?"
- **Outline spec**: "Transition: The analytical 1pPDF approach has been remarkably successful but operates under restrictive assumptions. The next section introduces a simulation-based alternative that relaxes these assumptions."
- **Problem**: The draft's transition asks a rhetorical question rather than stating the concrete issue (restrictive assumptions). The reader doesn't yet know *what* limitations the 1pPDF has, so the question floats without anchor.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace the rhetorical question with a statement that names the limitations (parametric model assumptions, computational cost of non-amortized convergence) before pointing to §6.3.

### Issue 3: §6.2.2 attribution still slightly misleading

- **Location**: §6.2.2, paragraph 4
- **Quote**: "Zechlin et al. \cite{Zechlin:2015wdz} improved the method significantly..."
- **Outline spec**: "Cuoco et al. (2015) [`Cuoco-1pdf`]: pixel-dependent improvement..."
- **Problem**: The outline attributes the pixel-dependent improvement to "Cuoco et al. (2015)" using bib key `Cuoco-1pdf`. While the paper's first author is Zechlin, the outline's convention uses the second author's name — likely because this is how the paper is commonly referenced in the author's own Paper 1. The internal bib key is literally `Cuoco-1pdf`. Using "Zechlin et al." creates a disconnect with the thesis's own reference convention.
- **Suggested fix**: Use the form "Zechlin, Cuoco et al." or follow the outline's convention "Cuoco et al." to avoid confusion when cross-referencing Paper 1's bibliography.

### Issue 4: §6.2.1 equation uses incorrect LaTeX environment

- **Location**: §6.2.1, lines 9–12
- **Problem**: The equation uses `$$...$$` (deprecated display math) with a `\label` inside. In LaTeX, `$$` does not support `\label` — this will compile but the label will not be referenceable.
- **Suggested fix**: Use `\begin{equation}...\end{equation}` instead.

### Issue 5: §6.2.2 equation same issue

- **Location**: §6.2.2, lines 9–12
- **Problem**: Same as Issue 4 — `$$` with `\label`.
- **Suggested fix**: Use `\begin{equation}...\end{equation}`.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 6: Missing "Euclidean norm" context for S⁻² (§6.2.1)

- **Location**: §6.2.1, final paragraph
- **Quote**: "broadly compatible with an $S^{-2}$ power law"
- **Problem**: The reader may not know why S⁻² is a natural reference. In a Euclidean universe with uniform source density, dN/dS ∝ S⁻⁵/² (the Euclidean expectation). The S⁻² behavior is shallower, indicating source evolution or a finite population. A brief clarifying remark would be helpful.
- **Suggested fix**: Add "(shallower than the Euclidean $S^{-5/2}$ expectation)" as a parenthetical.

### Issue 7: "over the past decade" is vague (§6.2.2)

- **Location**: §6.2.2, paragraph 1
- **Quote**: "has been developed and refined over the past decade"
- **Problem**: The pioneering paper (Malyshev & Hogg) is from 2011, now 15 years ago. "Over the past decade" is inaccurate.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace with "since its introduction in 2011" or "over the past fifteen years."

### Issue 8: First-person "we" in section intro

- **Location**: §6.2 intro
- **Quote**: "We begin with the formal definition..."
- **Problem**: Acceptable in thesis context, but the chapter outline's style notes say "scholarly, neutral tone." Check if the thesis uses "we" for roadmap sentences consistently.
- **Suggested fix**: No action if consistent with thesis conventions; otherwise replace with passive.

### Issue 9: UGRB acronym undefined in §6.2.3

- **Location**: §6.2.3, paragraph 2
- **Quote**: "the total contribution of unresolved point sources to the UGRB"
- **Problem**: UGRB was introduced in §6.2.1 as "unresolved gamma-ray background," but not with the explicit abbreviation "(UGRB)." The acronym first appears without definition.
- **Suggested fix**: Introduce the abbreviation explicitly at first use in §6.2.1: "...the unresolved gamma-ray background (UGRB)."
