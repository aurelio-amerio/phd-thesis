## 6.3 Simulation-Based Inference for $dN/dS$
\label{sec:6.3}

The previous section established $dN/dS$ as the central observable encoding the statistical properties of the sub-threshold source population, and introduced the 1pPDF as the primary analytical tool for its recovery.  We now turn to the methodological core of this chapter: a simulation-based inference approach that replaces the analytical likelihood with a convolutional neural network trained on synthetic Fermi-LAT maps.  We first discuss the limitations of the analytical framework that motivate this alternative (\S\ref{sec:6.3.1}), then describe the CNN-based methodology and its error estimation strategy (\S\ref{sec:6.3.2}), and finally present the \texttt{map2patches} algorithm for handling the spherical geometry of the data (\S\ref{sec:6.3.3}).
