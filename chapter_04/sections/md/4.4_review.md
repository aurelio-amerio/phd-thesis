## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 2: Em dash overuse (Humanizer pattern #13)
- **Location**: Throughout all subsubsections
- **Problem**: The section uses LaTeX em dashes (`---`) 11 times across ~2000 words. This is a high density and is flagged by the humanizer as AI pattern #13. While a few are natural in academic prose, having more than ~3-4 per 1000 words reads algorithmically generated. Key instances to consider replacing:
  - 4.4.1 L8: "the fit --- leaving this..." → comma clause
  - 4.4.1 L10: "excess --- which is exactly..." → rewrite with "because this is exactly"
  - 4.4.3 L4: "Unlike the original NPTF --- which discarded..." → ", which discarded"
  - 4.4.3 L15: "NPTF's strong Bayes factor and the inferred SCD peaking near the detection threshold --- is substantially weakened" → comma
- **Suggested fix**: Replace at least 4-5 em dashes with commas, parentheses, or restructured clauses. Keep em dashes only where they create a genuine emphatic pause.

### Issue 3: Sentence "The underlying mechanism is straightforward" is near-redundant
- **Location**: Section 4.4.1, paragraph 4 (line 10)
- **Quote**: "The underlying mechanism is straightforward: when the smooth template cannot capture the true spatial variance in the data, the resulting excess pixel-to-pixel scatter is mathematically equivalent to the signature of a point-source population."
- **Problem**: This sentence restates what was already explained in the preceding sentence ("will generate excess pixel-to-pixel scatter that the NPTF interprets as point-source emission"). The two sentences say the same thing in nearly identical words.
- **Suggested fix**: Delete the redundant sentence entirely. The point is already made.

### Issue 4: Weak integration of Cholis et al. in 4.4.2
- **Location**: Section 4.4.2, paragraph 3 (line 8)
- **Problem**: The Cholis et al. high-energy tail paragraph feels parachuted into the morphological ambiguity section. The section title is "The Morphological Ambiguity" but the Cholis argument is spectral, not morphological. While the chapter outline places it here, the in-text transition ("An independent line of evidence complicates the MSP interpretation from the spectral side") is abrupt.
- **Suggested fix**: Strengthen the transitional sentence to explicitly justify why a spectral argument appears in a morphological section, e.g.: "Beyond the morphological debate, which remains unresolved at the spatial level, an independent spectral challenge further complicates the astrophysical interpretation."

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 5: "of order a few hundred sources" — imprecise
- **Location**: Section 4.4.3, paragraph 2 (line 6)
- **Quote**: "typically requiring of order a few hundred sources"
- **Problem**: The NPTF (Lee et al. 2016) predicted ~200-400 sources; the energy-independent CNN preferred ~3-4 photons/source but didn't specify a total count as clearly. The phrase is vague.
- **Suggested fix**: "typically predicting populations of a few hundred sources (the 2016 NPTF preferred approximately 200)"

### Issue 6: "powerful additional lever arm" — hedging toward promotional
- **Location**: Section 4.4.3, paragraph 1 (line 4)
- **Quote**: "providing a powerful additional lever arm"
- **Problem**: "Powerful" is mildly promotional (humanizer pattern #4). The List et al. paper itself uses "lever arm" which is fine, but "powerful" inflates it.
- **Suggested fix**: "providing an additional lever arm"

### Issue 7: "has a dramatic effect" — evaluative
- **Location**: Section 4.4.3, paragraph 2 (line 6)
- **Quote**: "has a dramatic effect"
- **Problem**: "Dramatic" is evaluative. Let the data speak.
- **Suggested fix**: "substantially alters" or "profoundly alters"

### Issue 8: "called the reliability... into serious question" — slightly informal
- **Location**: Section 4.4.1, paragraph 1 (line 4)
- **Quote**: "called the reliability of these results into serious question"
- **Problem**: The idiom "called into question" is fine, but "into serious question" adds unnecessary emphasis.
- **Suggested fix**: "called the reliability of these results into question"

### Issue 9: References.md needs updating for Calore:2021jvg
- **Location**: `references.md` line 52
- **Quote**: `Calore et al. (2021) | — | — | Rebuttal: MSPs still preferred [check bib]`
- **Problem**: Bib key is now confirmed as `Calore:2021jvg` with arXiv `2102.12497`.
