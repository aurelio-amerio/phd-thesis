## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Dense paragraph in 3.5.1 — window functions block too long
- **Location**: Section 3.5.1, paragraph starting "The window functions are the central ingredients..."
- **Problem**: A single paragraph handles four distinct topics: (1) the galaxy window function, (2) historical attribution (Ando & Komatsu, Fornengo & Regis), (3) the DM annihilation window function equation, and (4) the physical interpretation (redshift peaking, contrast with blazars). This is too dense for comfortable reading.
- **Suggested fix**: Split into two paragraphs: (1) galaxy window function + historical context, (2) DM window function equation + decay contrast + physical interpretation.

### Issue 2: Missing "collective/statistical" precision in introduction paragraph 2
- **Location**: Section 3.5 introduction, paragraph 2
- **Problem**: The text should emphasize that the cross-correlation probes a *collective, statistical* signal from the aggregate of unresolved halos, not individual sources. The outline specifies "collective dark matter signal."
- **Suggested fix**: Rephrase to clarify the statistical nature of the signal.
