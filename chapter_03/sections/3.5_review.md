## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Dense paragraphs in 3.5.1 — window functions and halo model conflated
- **Location**: Section 3.5.1, paragraphs 5-6 (lines 28-38)
- **Quote**: "The window functions are the central ingredients... The angular power spectrum from annihilating dark matter was first formulated by Ando and Komatsu... For dark matter annihilation, the gamma-ray window function takes the form... For decaying dark matter... The annihilation window function is strongly peaked..."
- **Problem**: A single paragraph covers the galaxy window function, historical attribution for Ando & Komatsu and Fornengo & Regis, the full annihilation window function equation, the decay case, and the physical interpretation of redshift distributions. At ~130 words before the equation + ~100 words after, this is too dense for comfortable reading. The halo model paragraph that follows is fine on its own.
- **Suggested fix**: Split into two paragraphs: (1) galaxy window function + historical attribution, (2) DM annihilation/decay window function + physical interpretation.

### Issue 2: Missing explicit mention of "unresolved" gamma-ray background
- **Location**: Section 3.5 introduction, paragraph 2
- **Quote**: "one can isolate a faint, diffuse dark matter contribution that would be undetectable in either dataset alone"
- **Problem**: The chapter outline specifies that cross-correlations target the "collective dark matter signal from unresolved cosmological structure." The section intro mentions "unresolved" in paragraph 1 but paragraph 2 fails to distinguish resolved vs. unresolved when discussing the DM contribution. For a reader encountering this for the first time, it should be clear that the cross-correlation formalism specifically targets unresolved emission — the average clustering signal, not individual sources.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a clarifier that the cross-correlation probes the statistical average of unresolved cosmological structure.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 3: "parametrised" spelling
- **Location**: Section 3.5.1, line 4
- **Problem**: British spelling "parametrised" vs. the American "parameterized." The chapter_02 LaTeX uses American English conventions throughout.
- **Suggested fix**: Change to "parameterized" for consistency.

### Issue 4: "normalised" spelling
- **Location**: Section 3.5.1, lines 10 and 28
- **Problem**: British "normalised" inconsistent with American English conventions used in chapter_02.
