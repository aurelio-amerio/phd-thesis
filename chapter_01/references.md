# Chapter 1: The Dark Matter Problem - References & Sources

## 1. Reviews & Textbooks
*General consensus, theoretical foundations, and state-of-the-art summaries.*

### Cirelli, Strumia, & Zupan (2024/2025) - "Dark Matter"
*   **Source:** *arXiv:2406.01705*
*   **Relevance:** The most comprehensive modern review, structuring evidence across all scales. It explicitly defines the **WIMP paradigm** and the current "crisis" of non-detection. It provides updated global fits for the DM density and details the "Unitarity Bound" for thermal relics, serving as the primary theoretical baseline for the thesis.

### Hooper (2024) - "Particle Cosmology and Astrophysics"
*   **Source:** Textbook/Lecture Notes
*   **Relevance:** Connects historical evidence to modern particle physics. It details the **Zwicky cluster observations** and the derivation of the **Boltzmann equation**. This source is crucial for the "Pedagogical" sections of Chapter 1, specifically for deriving the freeze-out condition $n \langle \sigma v \rangle \sim H$ and the resulting relic abundance.

### Dodelson - "Modern Cosmology"
*   **Source:** Textbook
*   **Relevance:** The definitive mathematical text for the **$\Lambda$CDM model**. It provides the formal derivation of how Cold Dark Matter (CDM) drives the growth of structure (linear perturbation theory) and how its specific density ($\Omega_c h^2 \approx 0.12$) is required to fit the **CMB acoustic peaks** observed by Planck.

### Pinetti (2021) - "Thesis"
*   **Source:** PhD Thesis
*   **Relevance:** A key breakdown of the **Indirect Detection formalism** in a thesis context. It demonstrates how to structure the argument for WIMP annihilation signatures (gamma-rays) and provides a recent example of framing the "WIMP Miracle" against the backdrop of null results from direct detection.

## 2. Key Specific Papers
*Primary sources for specific claims, historical limits, and experimental results.*

*   **Rubin & Ford (1970):** *Rotation of the Andromeda Nebula...*
    *   **Source:** *ApJ 159:379*
    *   **Relevance:** The seminal optical study of M31 proving that rotational velocities remain constant at large radii. This finding implied that mass increases linearly with radius ($M(r) \propto r$), providing the first robust evidence for **extended dark halos** around spiral galaxies.

*   **Planck Collaboration (2018):** *VI. Cosmological parameters*
    *   **Source:** *arXiv:1807.06209*
    *   **Relevance:** Established the definitive cosmological parameters for the $\Lambda$CDM model. It determined the physical dark matter density to high precision ($\Omega_c h^2 = 0.120 \pm 0.001$), confirming that DM constitutes $\sim 26\%$ of the Universe's energy budget and validating the 6-parameter model.

*   **Lee & Weinberg (1977):** *Cosmological Lower Bound on Heavy Neutrino Masses*
    *   **Source:** *Phys. Rev. Lett. 39, 165*
    *   **Relevance:** The founding paper of the "WIMP Miracle." It calculated that heavy stable neutrinos (WIMPs) must have a mass $m > 2$ GeV to avoid overclosing the Universe ($\Omega > 1$). This established the relationship between weak-scale masses and the correct relic abundance.

*   **Steigman, Dasgupta, & Beacom (2012):** *Precise Relic WIMP Abundance...*
    *   **Source:** *arXiv:1204.3622*
    *   **Relevance:** The modern update to the freeze-out calculation. It precisely determined the "canonical" thermal annihilation cross-section required for the observed density: $\langle \sigma v \rangle \approx 2.2 \times 10^{-26} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s}$. This value is the standard benchmark for all indirect detection exclusion plots.

*   **Tremaine & Gunn (1979):** *Dynamical Role of Light Neutral Leptons*
    *   **Source:** *Phys. Rev. Lett. 42, 407*
    *   **Relevance:** Ruled out light standard model neutrinos as Dark Matter. By applying the **Pauli Exclusion Principle** to phase space in dwarf galaxies, they showed that fermionic DM must be heavier than a few hundred eV to cluster on such small scales, necessitating a new particle candidate.

*   **Fermi-LAT (Ackermann et al. 2015):** *Searching for Dark Matter Annihilation from Milky Way Dwarf Spheroidal Galaxies...*
    *   **Source:** *arXiv:1503.02641*
    *   **Relevance:** The standard-bearer for dSph constraints. Using 6 years of Fermi-LAT data from 15 dwarfs, it excluded the **thermal relic cross-section** for WIMP masses below $\sim 100$ GeV in the $b\bar{b}$ channel. This is the primary result challenging the simplest WIMP interpretations.
