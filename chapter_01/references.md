# Chapter 1: The Dark Matter Problem — References & Sources

## 1. Reviews & Textbooks
*General consensus, theoretical foundations, and state-of-the-art summaries.*

### Cirelli, Strumia, & Zupan (2024/2025) — "Dark Matter"
*   **Source:** *arXiv:2406.01705*
*   **Relevance:** The most comprehensive modern review, structuring evidence across all scales. It explicitly defines the **WIMP paradigm** and the current "crisis" of non-detection. It provides updated global fits for the DM density and details the "Unitarity Bound" for thermal relics, serving as the primary theoretical baseline for the thesis. Its "What" (Ch. 3) and "When" (Ch. 4) structure organizes candidates by mass scale/interaction type and production mechanism, respectively. Its indirect detection chapter (Ch. 6) follows a "Particle-to-Astrophysics Pipeline" structure.

### Hooper (2024) — "Particle Cosmology and Astrophysics"
*   **Source:** Textbook/Lecture Notes
*   **Relevance:** Connects historical evidence to modern particle physics. It details the **Zwicky cluster observations** and the derivation of the **Boltzmann equation**. Crucial for the "Pedagogical" sections, specifically for deriving the freeze-out condition $n \langle \sigma v \rangle \sim H$ and the resulting relic abundance. Uses a "Probe-Based" indirect detection structure.

### Dodelson — "Modern Cosmology"
*   **Source:** Textbook
*   **Relevance:** The definitive mathematical text for the **$\Lambda$CDM model**. It provides the formal derivation of how Cold Dark Matter (CDM) drives the growth of structure (linear perturbation theory) and how its specific density ($\Omega_c h^2 \approx 0.12$) is required to fit the **CMB acoustic peaks** observed by Planck.

### Pinetti (2021) — "Thesis"
*   **Source:** PhD Thesis
*   **Relevance:** A key breakdown of the **Indirect Detection formalism** in a thesis context. It demonstrates how to structure the argument for WIMP annihilation signatures (gamma-rays) and provides a recent example of framing the "WIMP Miracle" against the backdrop of null results from direct detection. Contains detailed treatment of J-factors, D-factors, and boost factors.

### Bullock & Boylan-Kolchin (2017) — "Small-Scale Challenges to the ΛCDM Paradigm"
*   **Source:** *arXiv:1707.04256*
*   **Relevance:** Comprehensive review of the "Small Scale Crisis": cusp-core problem, missing satellites, the diversity problem, too-big-to-fail. Reviews solutions from baryonic feedback, SIDM, and fuzzy DM. Directly relevant if addressing the small-scale tensions in Sec. 1.1.

## 2. Key Specific Papers
*Primary sources for specific claims, historical limits, and experimental results.*

### Evidence (Section 1.1)
*   **Rubin & Ford (1970):** *Rotation of the Andromeda Nebula...* — *ApJ 159:379*. Seminal optical study of M31 proving flat rotation curves → extended dark halos.
*   **Persic, Salucci & Stel (1996):** *Universal Rotation Curve* — *arXiv:astro-ph/9506004*. Standard reference for rotation curves of spiral galaxies.
*   **Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert (2016):** *SPARC* — *arXiv:1606.09251*. Modern compilation of 175 disk galaxies confirming ubiquitous flat rotation curves.
*   **Walker et al. (2009):** *A Universal Mass Profile for Dwarf Spheroidal Galaxies* — *arXiv:0904.1274*. Mass-to-light ratios in dSphs via velocity dispersions.
*   **Wolf et al. (2010):** *Accurate masses for dispersion-supported galaxies* — *arXiv:0908.2995*. Mass estimator for dSphs from line-of-sight velocity dispersions.
*   **Zwicky (1933):** *Die Rotverschiebung von extragalaktischen Nebeln* — *Helv. Phys. Acta 6, 110*. First application of Virial Theorem to galaxy clusters.
*   **Clowe et al. (2006):** *A Direct Empirical Proof of the Existence of Dark Matter* — *arXiv:astro-ph/0608407*. The Bullet Cluster: separation of collisionless DM from collisional baryons.
*   **Harvey et al. (2015):** *The nongravitational interactions of dark matter in colliding galaxy clusters* — *arXiv:1503.07675*. 72 cluster collisions → $>7\sigma$ existence of collisionless DM.
*   **Markevitch et al. (2004):** *Direct Constraints on the DM Self-Interaction Cross Section from the Merging Galaxy Cluster 1E 0657-56* — *arXiv:astro-ph/0309303*. Bullet Cluster X-ray constraints on DM self-interaction.
*   **Planck Collaboration (2018):** *VI. Cosmological parameters* — *arXiv:1807.06209*. Definitive $\Lambda$CDM parameters: $\Omega_c h^2 = 0.120 \pm 0.001$.
*   **Planck Collaboration (2020):** *VIII. Gravitational lensing* — *arXiv:1807.06210*. CMB lensing as ultimate weak lensing application.
*   **Eisenstein et al. (2005):** *Detection of the Baryon Acoustic Peak* — *arXiv:astro-ph/0501171*. First detection of BAO as standard ruler in SDSS.
*   **Riess et al. (1998):** *Observational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe* — *arXiv:astro-ph/9805201*. Discovery of accelerating expansion.
*   **Perlmutter et al. (1999):** *Measurements of Ω and Λ from 42 High-Redshift Supernovae* — *arXiv:astro-ph/9812133*. Independent SN Ia acceleration measurement.
*   **Scolnic et al. (2018):** *The Pantheon Sample* — *arXiv:1710.00845*. Modern SN Ia compilation.
*   **Navarro, Frenk & White (1997):** *A Universal Density Profile* — *arXiv:astro-ph/9611107*. The NFW profile from CDM simulations.

### Particle Physics (Section 1.2)
*   **Lee & Weinberg (1977):** *Cosmological Lower Bound on Heavy Neutrino Masses* — *Phys. Rev. Lett. 39, 165*. Lower bound on WIMP mass ($m > 2$ GeV).
*   **Tremaine & Gunn (1979):** *Dynamical Role of Light Neutral Leptons* — *Phys. Rev. Lett. 42, 407*. Phase-space argument excluding light neutrinos as DM.
*   **Cowsik & McClelland (1972):** *An Upper Limit on the Neutrino Rest Mass* — *Phys. Rev. Lett. 29*. Cosmological bound on neutrino mass.
*   **Steigman, Dasgupta & Beacom (2012):** *Precise Relic WIMP Abundance* — *arXiv:1204.3622*. Modern canonical cross-section: $\langle \sigma v \rangle \approx 2.2 \times 10^{-26}$ cm$^3$/s.
*   **Griest & Kamionkowski (1990):** *Unitarity Limits on the Mass and Radius of DM Particles* — *Phys. Rev. Lett. 64, 615*. Upper mass bound $m_\chi \lesssim 100$ TeV.
*   **Hall et al. (2010):** *Freeze-In Production of FIMP Dark Matter* — *arXiv:0911.1120*. Founding paper of the Freeze-in mechanism.
*   **Hisano et al. (2007):** *Non-perturbative Sommerfeld effect* — *arXiv:hep-ph/0610249*. Sommerfeld enhancement of thermal relic annihilation.
*   **Arkani-Hamed et al. (2009):** *A Theory of Dark Matter* — *arXiv:0810.0713*. Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation unified framework.
*   **Gondolo & Gelmini (1991):** *Cosmic abundances of stable particles* — *Nucl. Phys. B 360*. Standard freeze-out calculation.
*   **Ellis et al. (1984):** *Supersymmetric relic abundances* — *Nucl. Phys. B 238*. Neutralinos as DM, co-annihilation concept.
*   **Cirelli, Fornengo & Strumia (2006):** *Minimal Dark Matter* — *arXiv:hep-ph/0512090*. Minimal DM model from gauge quantum numbers.
*   **Feng & Kumar (2008):** *WIMPless Dark Matter* — *arXiv:0803.4196*. Models where DM interacts via non-weak forces.

### Indirect Detection (Section 1.3)
*   **Fermi-LAT (Ackermann et al. 2015):** *Searching for DM in dSphs* — *arXiv:1503.02641*. Standard dSph constraints excluding thermal cross-section for $m < 100$ GeV ($b\bar{b}$).
*   **Ibarra, Tran & Weniger (2013):** *Decaying Dark Matter* — *arXiv:1307.6434*. Comprehensive review of decaying DM.
*   **Bringmann et al. (2008):** *Virtual Internal Bremsstrahlung* — *arXiv:0710.3169*. VIB spectral features near kinematic endpoint.
*   **Ciafaloni et al. (2010):** *Electroweak Corrections to DM annihilation* — *arXiv:1009.0224*. EW bremsstrahlung for TeV-scale WIMPs.
*   **Springel et al. (2008):** *The Aquarius Project* — *arXiv:0809.0898*. High-resolution simulation of DM substructure and boost factor.
*   **Bonnivard et al. (2015):** *J-factor determination for dSphs* — *arXiv:1504.02048*. Robust J-factor uncertainties.
*   **Bergström, Ullio & Buckley (1998):** *Gamma-ray observability from neutralino annihilation* — *arXiv:astro-ph/9712318*. Classical indirect detection predictions.
*   **Bringmann (2009):** *Substructure boost factors* — *arXiv:0903.0189*. Theoretical estimates of clumping boost factors.
*   **Cirelli et al. (PPPC 4 DM ID) (2011):** Standard spectra tables used in the field.
*   **Daylan et al. (2016):** *The GCE characterization* — *arXiv:1402.6703*. Characterization of the Galactic Center gamma-ray signal.

### Multi-Messenger (Section 1.3.4)
*   **IceCube (2017):** *Search for DM annihilation in the Sun* — *arXiv:1612.05949*. Neutrino constraints on DM in the Sun.
*   **PAMELA (2009):** *Anomalous positron abundance* — *arXiv:0810.4995*. Positron excess in cosmic rays.
*   **Cuoco et al. (2017):** *Novel DM constraints from antiprotons* — *arXiv:1610.03071*. Antiproton constraints using AMS-02.
*   **Donato et al. (2008):** *Antideuterons as a probe of supersymmetric dark matter* — *arXiv:0803.2640*. Anti-nuclei as background-free DM window.
