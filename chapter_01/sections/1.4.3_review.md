## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Cusp-core paragraph length
- **Location**: Paragraph starting "The disagreement between cuspy simulations..."
- **Problem**: This is a single paragraph spanning ~15 typeset lines. It packs three stellar-to-halo mass ratio regimes, two particle physics resolutions, one astrophysical resolution, and the MW prediction into one block. The density of information makes it hard to parse on first reading.
- **Suggested fix**: Split into two paragraphs — one for the resolutions (SIDM, fuzzy DM, baryonic feedback) and one for the stellar-to-halo mass ratio regimes and the MW prediction. This improves readability without adding length.

### Issue 2: Missing Burkert original citation
- **Location**: Burkert profile paragraph
- **Problem**: The Burkert profile was introduced by Burkert (1995). The current text cites only `Cirelli:2024ssz` and `Bullock:2017xww` (reviews). The original paper should be cited for the profile definition, matching the treatment of NFW which cites `Navarro:1996gj`.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `\cite{Burkert:1995tz}` (or equivalent bib key) after "Burkert (cored)".
