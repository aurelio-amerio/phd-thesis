## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Overcounting of EW correction consequences
- **Location**: Electroweak corrections paragraph, line 47
- **Quote**: "First, the sharp peaks of monochromatic lines are smeared [...] Second, purely leptonic channels ($\chi\chi \to e^+e^-$, $\mu^+\mu^-$) and even the $\gamma\gamma$ channel acquire an antiproton yield..."
- **Problem**: The phrase "The consequences are twofold" sets up exactly two points, but this is a common AI structural pattern (rule-of-two variant). While the content is correct, the rigid enumeration feels slightly formulaic.
- **Suggested fix**: Rephrase to soften the enumeration — e.g., replace "The consequences are twofold." with a less mechanical transition. However, in physics writing this is a standard convention, so this is borderline. **No change applied** — left for author judgment.

### Issue 2: Long compound sentence in secondary emission paragraph
- **Location**: Last sentence of the section, line 51
- **Quote**: "the very identification of the excess relies on subtracting the diffuse gamma-ray background dominated by cosmic-ray ICS and bremsstrahlung in the dense inner Galaxy \cite{...}, and ICS constraints from peaked density profiles have been used to exclude leptophilic dark matter interpretations \cite{...}."
- **Problem**: This sentence carries two independent claims joined by "and". Each deserves its own sentence for clarity.
- **Suggested fix**: Split into two sentences. Applied below.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 3: Inconsistent terminology — "loop factor" vs "$\alpha^2$"
- **Location**: Monochromatic lines paragraph, line 43
- **Quote**: "suppressing the cross-section by a loop factor"
- **Problem**: The original draft cited "$\alpha^2$" as the suppression factor, which is more specific. The current version says "a loop factor" which is vaguer. The NotebookLM source (Cirelli) says "often reduced by a loop factor" without specifying $\alpha^2$. Since Cirelli does not give a universal numerical factor, "loop factor" is more accurate.
- **Suggested fix**: No change needed — the current wording is correct per the source.

### Issue 4: Missing reference for Bergström et al. on VIB
- **Location**: VIB paragraph, line 45
- **Quote**: "A second sharp feature arises when a photon is radiated from a virtual charged particle mediating the annihilation \cite{Bringmann:2007nk}."
- **Problem**: Bergström's 1989 paper (`Bergstrom:1989jr`) is the original VIB calculation, with Bringmann et al. 2008 being the follow-up that highlighted its importance for dark matter. The current citation to `Bringmann:2007nk` is the standard modern reference and is appropriate for a thesis introduction.
- **Suggested fix**: No change needed — `Bringmann:2007nk` is the standard reference.

### Issue 5: Missing mention of "box-shaped" spectral features
- **Location**: After VIB paragraph
- **Problem**: Cirelli fig. 6.6 also shows "top-hat" and "single saw-tooth" features from cascade annihilations ($\chi\chi \to XX \to \gamma\gamma\gamma\gamma$). These are less commonly searched for but exist in the literature.
- **Suggested fix**: These are niche features not directly relevant to the thesis analyses. **No change needed** — the current coverage (lines, VIB, EW) is sufficient for a thesis introduction.

### Issue 6: Style — "As a practical rule of thumb"
- **Location**: Secondary emission paragraph, line 51
- **Problem**: "As a practical rule of thumb" is slightly informal for thesis prose. Could be "Quantitatively" or "To fix orders of magnitude".
- **Suggested fix**: Replace with "To fix orders of magnitude" for a cleaner physics register. Applied below.
