## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Citation granularity — over-reliance on reviews
- **Location**: Entire section
- **Problem**: Nearly every citation is `\cite{Cirelli:2024ssz}` or `\cite{Hooper:2024}`. The chapter outline and `references.md` specify original experimental papers that should be cited directly:
  - **LZ**: Aalbers et al. 2023 (arXiv:2207.03993) — currently not cited
  - **XENONnT**: Aprile et al. 2023 — currently not cited
  - **PandaX-4T**: Meng et al. 2021 — currently not cited
  - **Fermi-LAT dSph limits**: Ackermann et al. 2015 (arXiv:1503.02641) — currently not cited, despite being listed in `references.md` as a key reference
  - **GCE characterization**: Daylan et al. 2016 (arXiv:1402.6703) — should be cited alongside the reviews
  - **Boveia & Doglioni (2018)**: LHC DM Working Group simplified models — mentioned in outline but not cited
- **Suggested fix**: Add original-paper citations alongside the review citations. Use `\cite{Aalbers:2022zts,Cirelli:2024ssz}` format. At minimum, add the 6 papers listed above.

### Issue 2: Missing content — annual modulation / DAMA
- **Location**: Section 1.3.1
- **Quote**: (absent)
- **Problem**: The chapter outline specifies: *"Annual modulation: Earth's orbital motion modulates the WIMP flux by ~5% (Drukier, Freese & Spergel 1986). DAMA/LIBRA observes a persistent modulation signal; its interpretation remains debated."* This is entirely absent from the draft.
- **Suggested fix**: Add 1–2 sentences on annual modulation as a detection signature and the DAMA controversy, either in the kinematics paragraph or after the TPC discussion.

### Issue 3: Missing content — low-mass frontier detectors
- **Location**: Section 1.3.1
- **Problem**: The chapter outline mentions *"Cryogenic bolometers (SuperCDMS, CRESST-III) and charge-coupled devices (SENSEI, DAMIC) extend sensitivity to sub-GeV WIMPs"* and electron scattering for sub-GeV DM. The draft only mentions xenon TPCs and alludes to DM-electron scattering obliquely.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a brief sentence noting other detector technologies that extend reach below ~5 GeV.

### Issue 4: Inconsistency — SI limit value
- **Location**: Section 1.3.1, paragraph 3
- **Quote**: "$\sigma_\text{SI} \lesssim 10^{-46}$ cm$^2$ for a dark matter mass near $M_\chi \sim 90$ GeV"
- **Problem**: The chapter outline states "$\sigma_{SI} \lesssim 10^{-47}$ cm$^2$ at $m_\chi \sim 30$ GeV". The difference is the reference mass. The draft's value from the Cirelli review is at $M_\chi \sim M_Z \sim 90$ GeV, while the outline quotes the absolute minimum of the exclusion curve at ~30 GeV. Both are correct at different masses, but the draft should either quote the more impressive number at the curve minimum or clarify the mass dependence.
- **Suggested fix**: Use "$\sigma_\text{SI} \lesssim 10^{-47}$ cm$^2$ near $M_\chi \sim 30$ GeV (at the minimum of the exclusion curve)" and cite the original LZ/XENONnT papers.

### Issue 5: Missing "takeaway" sentences
- **Location**: End of 1.3.1 and 1.3.2
- **Problem**: The chapter outline specifies explicit takeaway sentences for each subsubsection (e.g., *"Direct detection sets the strongest constraints on the WIMP-nucleon coupling... but is insensitive to the annihilation cross-section"*). These functional summaries are present but weaker in the draft — the direct detection takeaway is implicit, and the collider takeaway is good but could be sharper.
- **Suggested fix**: Make the final sentence of 1.3.1 more explicitly connect to indirect detection: mention that direct detection measures the scattering cross-section but is insensitive to $\langle \sigma v \rangle$.

### Issue 6: `\cite{Pinetti:2022}` key needs verification
- **Location**: Lines 46, 48, 50
- **Problem**: The Pinetti thesis in the NotebookLM notebook is arXiv:2212.00125. The bib key `Pinetti:2022` may not match the thesis `.bib` file. Need to verify or use the arXiv-derived key (likely `Pinetti:2022hkp` or similar).
- **Suggested fix**: Check the `.bib` file and correct the key.

### Issue 7: Missing explicit mention of DARWIN/XLZD
- **Location**: Section 1.3.1
- **Problem**: The outline mentions "Future ton-scale detectors (DARWIN/XLZD) will approach this limit" for the neutrino fog discussion. The draft describes the neutrino fog but doesn't name the next-generation experiments.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a brief mention of DARWIN/XLZD as the future program that will enter the neutrino fog.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 8: "slam particles together"
- **Location**: Intro, paragraph 2
- **Quote**: "Colliders, by contrast, slam particles together at energies..."
- **Problem**: Slightly informal for thesis prose. Acceptable in a textbook but may draw a referee's eye.
- **Suggested fix**: "Colliders, by contrast, collide particles at energies..." or keep if desired for readability.

### Issue 9: "how do we actually detect it?"
- **Location**: Intro, paragraph 1
- **Quote**: "the question becomes: how do we actually detect it?"
- **Problem**: The rhetorical question with "actually" is mildly conversational. Fine for readability, but a referee might prefer a more formal construction.
- **Suggested fix**: "the question becomes how to detect it experimentally" or keep as-is.

### Issue 10: Redundancy with Section 1.2 ending
- **Location**: Section 1.3.3, paragraph 1
- **Quote**: "$\langle \sigma v_\text{rel} \rangle \approx 2.2 \times 10^{-26}$ cm$^3$/s"
- **Problem**: This thermal relic value is stated in the intro, again in 1.3.3, and was also derived in Section 1.2. Three repetitions in quick succession.
- **Suggested fix**: In 1.3.3, reference the value derived earlier rather than restating it: "the thermal relic value derived in Section~\ref{sec:1.2.2}".

### Issue 11: Bold in markdown → LaTeX
- **Location**: Throughout (e.g., **spin-independent (SI)**, **mono-X searches**)
- **Problem**: These bold terms in the `.md` draft will need to be converted to `\textbf{}` or `\emph{}` in the LaTeX version. This is a formatting reminder, not a content issue.

### Issue 12: Missing reference to O'Hare (2021)
- **Location**: Neutrino fog paragraph
- **Problem**: The outline references "O'Hare 2021" for the neutrino fog discussion. Currently not cited.
- **Suggested fix**: Add `\cite{OHare:2021utq}` or equivalent.
