## Important Issues (🟡)

### Issue 1: Slight redundancy with Section 1.4.6 on positrons/antiprotons
- **Location**: Section 1.4.7, "Unresolved anomalies" paragraph
- **Quote**: "The earlier positron excess measured by PAMELA and AMS-02 above 10 GeV…is now widely attributed to nearby pulsars"
- **Problem**: Section 1.4.6 already discusses the positron excess attribution and antiproton consistency in detail. The 1.4.7 treatment partially repeats this information. Some readers may notice the overlap.
- **Suggested fix**: Keep the mentions in 1.4.7 but shorten them with a back-reference: "As discussed in Section 1.4.6, the positron excess…" This avoids full repetition while maintaining the narrative flow of the status summary.
- **Status**: ✅ Fixed — added cross-reference phrasing.

### Issue 2: Missing explicit mention of what parameter space remains open
- **Location**: Section 1.4.7, "The methodological case" paragraph
- **Quote**: "Yet the thermal cross-section remains within reach for heavier dark matter candidates, leptonic channels, and velocity-dependent models."
- **Problem**: The original draft (pre-revision) had a full paragraph explaining why null results are not the end of the story — the thermal relic benchmark is a cosmological target, not a model prediction; $J$-factor uncertainties affect interpretations; etc. The revised version compresses this into a single sentence, losing some of the nuance.
- **Suggested fix**: Expand slightly to re-include the key argument that the thermal cross-section is a cosmological target, not a model-specific prediction. One additional sentence would suffice.
- **Status**: ✅ Fixed — added clarifying sentence.

## Minor Issues (🟢)

### Issue 3: "landscape" usage
- **Location**: "these results define the landscape in which this thesis operates"
- **Problem**: The word "landscape" is on the humanizer watch list. While it is used here in a concrete sense (parameter space landscape), it could be replaced.
- **Suggested fix**: Replace with "context" or "situation" or keep as-is since it is used concretely.
- **Status**: ✅ Fixed — changed to "context."

### Issue 4: Missing Ackermann et al. year in running text
- **Location**: Gamma-ray constraints paragraph
- **Problem**: The dSph analysis is cited as `\cite{Ackermann:2015tah}` but never named in running text (e.g., "Ackermann et al. 2015"). For a key result this prominent, a named citation improves readability.
- **Suggested fix**: Add parenthetical "(Ackermann et al. 2015)" or use "Ackermann et al. (2015)".
- **Status**: Left for user judgment — depends on thesis citation style.

### Issue 5: Bold headers within prose
- **Location**: All four sub-blocks use Markdown bold headers
- **Problem**: These render as inline bold in the final LaTeX — which is fine for a thesis, but the author should verify this matches other sections' formatting conventions.
- **Status**: Left for user — formatting is consistent with earlier subsections (1.4.3, 1.4.5).

### Issue 6: "this thesis implements exactly that transition"
- **Location**: Last methodological paragraph
- **Problem**: Slightly promotional tone. Consider softening to "The analyses of this thesis pursue that transition."
- **Status**: ✅ Fixed — softened phrasing.
