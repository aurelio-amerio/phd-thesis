## Cross-chapter patterns worth a global pass

1. **"robust" (overused AI word)** — appears in chapters 6 four times, plus scattered elsewhere. Consider auditing all instances thesis-wide.
2. **"Taken together, ..." as a forced-synthesis opener** — appears in chapters 4 (twice) and 5. It's in your own "recurring connectives" style list, but repeating it back-to-back within the same chapter reads as formulaic; vary it.
3. **Copula avoidance ("serves as", "represents", "constitutes", "act as") instead of plain "is/are"** — the single most common tell found, spread across chapters 1, 2, 4, 5, 6.
4. **Duplicated sentence** — "In our efforts to better understand the potential particle nature of dark matter, we now steer our attention towards indirect detection searches." appears verbatim in both `1.3_searching_for_dark_matter.tex` and `1.4_indirect_detection.tex`. Worth checking whether this is an intentional repeat or a copy-paste leftover.
5. **Inflated-significance adjectives ("fundamental", "crucial", "striking", "definitive", "exceptionally", "powerful")** — scattered across chapters 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, generally as unearned intensifiers before a noun.

No instances of "delve", "intricate/intricacies", "tapestry", "underscore", "showcase", "fostering", "garner", or "align with" were found anywhere in Chapters 1–8.
