# Section 1.4.2 "Spectral Features and Signatures" — Rewrite Design

**Target file:** `chapter_01/sections/1.4_indirect_detection.tex`
**Target lines:** 83–137 (the full `\subsection{Spectral Features and Signatures}` body)
**Source of issues:** 4 in-progress Review-Mode annotations from supervisor feedback (revisions 6–8).

## Problem

The subsection is too technical and too "particle physics"-like for a thesis focused on gamma-ray data analysis and ML methods. The supervisor's feedback across four annotations is consistent: the section is needed (it introduces spectral features that distinguish DM from astrophysical backgrounds), but the current framing reads like a DM model-building chapter rather than an observational-methods thesis.

The thesis analyses never use line searches, VIB, or EW corrections — they work with continuum spectra from `bb̄` and `τ⁺τ⁻` channels. The section should therefore present these features as an observer's catalog of "what to look for," not as a particle theorist's derivation of "why the cross-section has this form."

## Decisions (locked in)

### 1. Overall approach: restructure around observational discriminating power (Approach A)

Reorganize the subsection into two blocks:
1. **Sharp spectral features** — presented as a catalog of signatures with no astrophysical counterpart, with physical intuition but no model-building detail.
2. **Prompt vs. secondary emission** — kept largely as-is (supervisor explicitly endorsed this block).

### 2. Monochromatic lines: keep most content, simplify language

Keep as its own bold paragraph (~5–6 sentences):
- Line at `E = m_χ` from `χχ → γγ`. Loop-suppressed so faint, but no astrophysical mechanism produces this → smoking gun.
- Related processes (`γZ`, `γh`) produce displaced lines — describe in words, drop the explicit `E_γ = m_χ(1 - M²_{Z,h}/4m²_χ)` formula.
- Mention Sommerfeld exception briefly.
- Simplify language throughout; avoid propagator/loop-diagram vocabulary.

### 3. VIB: shrink to 2–3 sentences

Keep as a short bold paragraph:
- Name, saw-tooth shape, sharp feature near the kinematic endpoint.
- Can dominate in models where two-body annihilation is suppressed.
- Point to Bringmann et al. and Cirelli review for details.
- Drop: propagator-near-mass-shell physics, helicity suppression lifting, 3-body dominance mechanism.

### 4. EW corrections: trim to 2–3 sentences, lead with physical meaning

Keep as a short bold paragraph:
- At TeV masses, radiation of W/Z bosons smears sharp features and generates new low-energy continuum.
- Also mixes messenger channels (e.g., antiprotons from initially leptonic annihilation).
- Point to reviews for technical treatment.
- Drop: `ln(m_χ/M_W)` log-enhancement formula, "formally higher-order" framing, the specific claim about antiprotons from `γγ` channel, the "incorporated into spectral libraries only after 2013" historical note.

### 5. Figure: keep as-is

`fig:spectral_features` (Cirelli et al. cartoon) and its caption are untouched. The figure serves as a visual reference catalog — readers can see the shapes even though the text discussion is briefer.

### 6. Prompt vs. secondary emission: keep as-is

Lines 125–137 stay. Supervisor explicitly confirmed this block is important. Only minor citation adjustments if needed (already handled in earlier revision passes).
