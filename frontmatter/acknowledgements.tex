I would like to thank all of my collaborators for their guidance.

I thank Nicolao for his continued guidance and support throughout these years, together with Alessandro and the group from the University of Turin. I hope we will continue thinking about how to solve the most intriguing (or absurd) questions in physics.

I thank Elena, one of the people I admire the most, who throughout these years has always charted her own path forward, both in science and life, with strength, conviction, care and loyalty towards all the people she holds dear. Her example has been an inspiration.
